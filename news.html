Xi'an Broadcasting and TelevisionSeptember 6, 2025

First On-Orbit Validation of NPU's Flexible Spacecraft Sensing System

西工大实现航天器柔性传感系统首次在轨验证

Xi'an Broadcasting and Television reports on a flexible sensing system launched on September 5, 2025. Jointly developed by Prof. Haitao Yang's team and collaborators from NPU's School of Astronautics, the system monitors the deformation and attitude of satellite deployable structures. Its first on-orbit validation supports real-time state monitoring and structural health assessment for spacecraft.

From Science Fiction to Reality: Exoskeleton Robotics in Henan

从科幻走进现实，“河南造”外骨骼机器人竞逐千亿级新赛道

Dahe Cube interviews Prof. Haitao Yang about intelligent wearable soft exoskeletons. The project combines flexible strain sensing with intelligent algorithms for movement assistance, with potential applications in rehabilitation, elder care and industrial work. The report also discusses the path from laboratory prototypes to scalable production and partnerships across the supply chain.
